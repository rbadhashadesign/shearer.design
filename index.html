About Me.

I’m a graduating college student specialising in Fashion, Textiles and Costume Design, with a creative passion for prop making and cosplay for Comic Con. I design and present large-scale costume work for display at events, where my pieces are recognised for their quality and detail. Inspired by comics and character design, I create authentic costume pieces using both traditional techniques and 3D printing.
