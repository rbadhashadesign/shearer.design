Doom Slayer

I carefully built this Doomguy suit using a mix of 3D-printed parts for the detailed armor and foam for the main undersuit. I also added LEDs to the chest and helmet to give the costume more of a sci-fi feel and make it stand out.

Purpose

The entire suit was made in a limited time frame for Olympia Comic Con, so there are parts I would improve if I had more time, especially sanding and finishing some of the 3D-printed pieces. The suit is mainly designed for it's look but wasn't very comfortable to wear for long periods. With this in mind, I experimented by including a cooling fan in a vest underneath, to help make the costume more wearable.

Craft

For the paintwork, I used different shades of green inspired by the game. The helmet color matches the leg armor, while the thigh armor matches the darker tones on the shoulders to keep the design consistent.
