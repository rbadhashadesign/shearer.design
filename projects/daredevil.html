DareDevil

DareDevil

This Daredevil suit is one of my favourite builds. It’s mainly a screen-printed suit that I bought in separate pieces and then sewed together myself. Some parts were especially challenging, like working with the black paneling and the different fabric textures, but once I had mastered the technique, I was able to make steady progress. Because of this, the suit took quite a long time to complete.

Equipment

The mask and batons were 3D printed, while all of the latex parts were bought separately, as making them from scratch would have been very difficult. There are a few things I would change if I remade the suit, such as fixing areas that were not as fitted as I would have liked. I also wasn’t able to sand the mask as thoroughly as I would have liked, because the file was textured, and sanding it further would have damaged the detail.

Details

Equipment-The suit doesn’t have many accessories, with the batons being the main feature. However, the batons include a unique detail: they can be connected together to form a longer baton, just like in the show.
